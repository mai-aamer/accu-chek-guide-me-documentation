# Safety

## Safety Information

> [!NOTE]
> Follow the instructions in the user manual when you use the Accu-Chek Guide Me meter and the lancing device. Use them only for their intended purpose.

## Warnings and Precautions

### Warnings

> [!WARNING]
> - Never share the meter or the lancing device with anyone else.
> - Use a new sterile lancet for every test, and never reuse a lancet.
> - Throw used lancets away in a puncture-proof container.
> - Keep the meter, test strips, and lancing device out of the reach of children.

### Precautions

- Wash and dry your hands before every blood glucose test.
- Use only Accu-Chek Guide test strips with this meter.

### Lancing Device

- Do not press the release button while you are pressing the priming button.

### Test Strips

- Do not use a test strip after its [Use By date](getting-started.md#preparing-the-test-strip).
- Keep test strips in their original container with the cap closed tightly, and protect them from heat, moisture, and humidity.
- Use a test strip right after you take it out of the container.
- Do not put blood or control solution on a test strip before it is inserted into the meter.
- Do not bend, cut, or alter test strips.

### Meter

- Do not use the meter if it is damaged.
- If you drop the meter, run a control test before you use it again.
- Keep the meter clean, and protect it from excessive heat and moisture.
