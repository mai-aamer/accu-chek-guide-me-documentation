# User Guide

This guide explains how to perform a blood glucose test, understand the result, and review stored results. Before testing, prepare the meter and the test strip as described in [Getting Started](getting-started.md).

## Performing a Blood Glucose Test

### Preparing the Lancing Device

1. Pull the cap straight off the lancing device. Do not twist it.
2. Push a new lancet into the lancing device until it clicks into place.
3. Twist off the protective cap of the lancet.
4. Put the cap back on the lancing device. Line up the notch on the cap with the notch on the device.

### Setting the Penetration Depth

The comfort dial has a depth indicator that shows the current setting. A higher number means a deeper puncture.

> [!TIP]
> The best setting is the lowest number that still gives you enough blood for a test.

1. Turn the comfort dial to adjust the depth.
2. Push the priming button in as far as it goes, as you would click a pen. When the release button turns yellow, the lancing device is ready.

### Obtaining a Blood Drop

1. Wash your hands with warm, soapy water and dry them well.
2. When the flashing drop symbol appears, press the lancing device firmly against the side of your fingertip.
3. Press the yellow release button to prick your finger.
4. Gently squeeze your finger to help a drop of blood form.
5. Touch the yellow edge of the test strip to the blood drop. Do not put blood on top of the strip.
6. Take your finger away from the strip when the flashing hourglass symbol appears.

### Discarding the Used Lancet

1. Remove the cap from the lancing device.
2. Point the lancet away from yourself.
3. Slide out the ejector to drop the lancet into a puncture-proof container.
4. Dispose of the lancet according to your local regulations.
5. Wash your hands with soap and water and dry them well.

## Understanding Test Results

The meter can display blood glucose results in mg/dL or mmol/L. The unit of measurement is shown on the label on the back of the meter.

> [!WARNING]
> Using the wrong unit of measurement may cause you to misread your blood glucose level.

## Using Device Features

### Reviewing Stored Results

The meter automatically saves up to 720 blood glucose results, each with the date and time of the test. When the memory is full, each new result replaces the oldest one.

The meter also saves control results, but only the most recent control result can be viewed on the meter.

1. With the meter off, press the Left or Right Arrow button to view your stored blood glucose results.
2. The most recent result appears first.
3. Keep pressing the Left or Right Arrow button to move through earlier results, from newest to oldest.
