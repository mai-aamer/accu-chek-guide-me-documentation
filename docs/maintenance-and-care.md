# Maintenance and Care

Regular care keeps the meter, the lancing device, and the test strips in good working condition. For safety rules, see [Safety](safety.md).

## Cleaning

### Cleaning the Meter

Clean the meter regularly, following the instructions in the user manual. Keep the meter free from dirt, dust, blood, and other contaminants.

### Disinfecting the Meter

Disinfect the meter using the recommended procedure and follow the manufacturer's instructions.

> [!NOTE]
> For detailed cleaning and disinfecting steps, see the [official user manual](https://www.accu-chek.com/document/accu-chek-guide-me-users-manual-english/download?inline).

## Storage

### Meter and Lancing Device

> [!NOTE]
> Store the meter and the lancing device in a clean, dry place.

Protect the meter from excessive heat, moisture, and humidity.

### Test Strips

- Keep test strips in their original container. Do not move them to another container.
- Protect test strips from excessive heat, moisture, and humidity.
- Close the cap of the container tightly after you take out a test strip.
- Do not bend, cut, or alter test strips.
- Use test strips before their Use By date.

## Device Care

Handle the meter and the lancing device carefully.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not use a damaged meter or lancing device.
