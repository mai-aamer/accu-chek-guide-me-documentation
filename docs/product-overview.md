# Product Overview

## Overview

The Accu-Chek Guide Me is a blood glucose meter that helps users check their blood glucose level and keep track of how well their glucose is being managed.

## Intended Use

> [!NOTE]
> The meter measures glucose in fresh capillary whole blood. Users can collect the blood sample from the fingertip, palm, or upper arm.

## Key Features

- **Fast results:** The meter works with test strips and shows results in less than 4 seconds.
- **Bluetooth connectivity:** The meter connects to the mySugr app via Bluetooth.
- **Easy-to-read screen:** The display is designed to be clear and simple to read.
