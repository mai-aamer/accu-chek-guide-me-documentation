# Getting Started

## What You Need

Before you begin, make sure you have the following items:

- Accu-Chek Guide Me blood glucose meter
- Test strips
- Accu-Chek Softclix lancing device
- Lancets

## Preparing the Meter

### Setting the Time and Date

1. Briefly press the Power/Set button to switch the meter on.
2. Hold down the Power/Set button until `set-up` appears on the display. The hour starts to flash.
3. Press the Left or Right Arrow button to decrease or increase the hour.
4. Press the Power/Set button to confirm the hour and move to the next setting.
5. Repeat steps 3 and 4 to set the minutes, AM/PM, month, day, and year.
6. Hold down the Power/Set button until the flashing test strip symbol appears.

## Preparing the Test Strip

> [!WARNING]
> Do not use test strips that are past the Use By date.

1. Check the Use By date on the test strip container.
2. Take one test strip out of the container, then close the cap firmly.
3. Insert the metallic end of the test strip into the meter. Place the meter on a flat surface. The meter turns on and a flashing drop symbol appears.
